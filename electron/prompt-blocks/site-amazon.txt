DIRECT URLS — jump straight to the right page, never go via homepage:
- Search:          https://www.amazon.com/s?k=QUERY+HERE
- Product by ASIN: https://www.amazon.com/dp/ASIN
- Order history:   https://www.amazon.com/gp/css/order-history
- Cart:            https://www.amazon.com/gp/cart/view.html
- Track package:   https://www.amazon.com/gp/your-account/ship-track?ie=UTF8&itemId=ITEM_ID
- Returns:         https://www.amazon.com/gp/css/returns/homepage.html
- Account:         https://www.amazon.com/gp/css/homepage.html

PAGE TYPES & WHAT TO DO ON EACH:

▸ SEARCH RESULTS PAGE
- Use get_page_text to extract product names, prices, ratings, review counts quickly.
- Product price is usually near the product title — look for "$" symbol.
- Sponsored/ad results appear at the top — skip them for organic results.
- To sort: find the "Sort by" dropdown (select_option). Options: "price-asc-rank" = Price Low to High.
- Filters (left sidebar): brand, price range, Prime, avg customer review. Click to apply.
- Next page: button labeled "Next" or page numbers at the bottom.

▸ PRODUCT DETAIL PAGE
- Price: look for id="corePrice_feature_div" or class containing "a-price". Get the whole price, not just the cents.
- Variants (size, color, style): these are BUTTON groups or dropdowns. You MUST select the right variant before adding to cart. read_page filter="interactive" to find them.
- "Add to Cart" vs "Buy Now": Add to Cart is safer — never click "Buy Now" (that goes straight to checkout).
- Availability: look for "In Stock", "Usually ships in...", "Currently unavailable", or "Ships from / Sold by" text.
- Reviews: scroll down or navigate to https://www.amazon.com/product-reviews/ASIN for the full review list.
- Image zoom: not needed for agent tasks — skip.
- Coupons: some products show a checkbox "Apply coupon". Click it before Add to Cart.

▸ CART PAGE
- Each item shows: name, qty input, price, "Delete" link.
- Change qty: type_text in the quantity field, then click "Update".
- Remove item: click the "Delete" link for that item.
- Subtotal is at the top right in "Subtotal (N items): $X".

▸ CHECKOUT FLOW — NEVER COMPLETE WITHOUT EXPLICIT USER PERMISSION
- Checkout has multiple steps: shipping address → delivery speed → payment → place order.
- STOP before "Place your order" — that is the final commit. Pause and confirm with user.
- Fill address fields in sequence: Full Name, Address Line 1, City, State (dropdown), ZIP, Phone.
- Delivery options are radio buttons — read_page to find them, click the user's preferred one.
- Payment: never enter card details unless user explicitly provides them.

▸ ORDER HISTORY
- Navigate to order history URL above. Orders list with order number, date, total, status.
- Click an order to see items, tracking, invoice.
- get_page_text works well here for extracting order data.

▸ TRACKING
- "Track Package" links appear in order detail pages.
- Carrier tracking links (UPS, USPS, FedEx) may open in a new tab.

COMMON FAILURES & HOW TO FIX:
- "Sign in required" wall: stop and tell the user — do not try to sign in on their behalf.
- CAPTCHA: stop immediately, tell the user, do not attempt to solve.
- Price not visible on first read_page: call screenshot — price may be in a dynamic component. Or try get_page_text.
- Variant not selected error on "Add to Cart": go back, select ALL variant dropdowns first.
- Amazon frequently uses shadow DOM for ratings — get_page_text is more reliable than read_page for rating numbers.
- "Items in cart" badge doesn't update immediately on SPA — navigate to cart URL to confirm.

PRICE RESEARCH PATTERN (most efficient):
1. Navigate to search URL with the query.
2. get_page_text to extract all product names + prices in one call.
3. Sort the list, identify the top 3 options with price + rating.
4. Navigate to the best product's detail page to confirm availability and variants.

NEVER:
- Click "Place order", "Buy now" (when in checkout), or "1-Click Order" without explicit user approval.
- Enter payment details unless user provided them.
- Sign in on behalf of the user.
